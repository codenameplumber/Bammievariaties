About Bammie

Two stores, one Bammie, made with love.

Bammie Varieties Store keeps your home stocked with foodstuffs, provisions and stationery. Bammie Bake and Cook makes snacks, cakes and meals fresh to order. Both are run with the same care.

How Bammie works

A shop you can walk into, and a kitchen that cooks to order

Bammie Varieties Store is a real shop you can walk into for foodstuffs, provisions and a little stationery, plus our own Bammie Chips. Many customers send their list on WhatsApp or TikTok instead, and we pack it for delivery, for pickup, or for their own rider or van.

Bammie Bake and Cook works by pre-order. When a fresh batch of chin chin, meat pie or puff-puff is coming, it goes up on our WhatsApp status, and customers order for their weekend or their occasion. For birthdays, naming ceremonies and other events, we plan with you and send a quote.

This website brings both together, so you can see what we sell, order in a few taps, and send it straight to us on WhatsApp. No account, no app to download.
